Silk begins before the cocoon.

Team Kaushya is a student-led startup from Wisdom World School, Wakad. We build three linked, low-cost machines that help silk farmers mount, harvest and sort cocoons.

National innovation and startup competition · Theme 2: Empowering Hands

The journey of a silk thread

Mulberry silk comes from the silkworm Bombyx mori. Pick a stage to follow the thread. The gold stages are where Team Kaushya works.

  • One thread Each cocoon is a single continuous filament, often close to a kilometre long.
  • Only mulberry The worms feed on mulberry leaves, so farms grow leaf as well as silk.
  • Two proteins Fibroin forms the fibre. Sericin, the gum, glues it into a cocoon.

Three stages, three struggles

Our three machines

One machine for each stage, designed to work together.

Aakar mounting

A collapsible plastic chandrika that keeps the familiar hexagonal form. Flat honeycomb panels are joined by hinges and sit on a frame with triangular ridges.

  • Folds flat when removed, so it is easy to store
  • Aims for uniform cocoon size and shape

Nivara harvesting

A rotary-style harvester. The plastic chandrika stays static on a frame with triangular ridges, and a matching-triangle top panel presses it down.

  • A scraper plate with matching triangles pushes across
  • Cocoons drop into a collection tray

Vidura sorting

A sensor-based sorter that checks one cocoon at a time and separates good from bad.

  • Hopper with an air-lock gate
  • 3 TCS34725 colour sensors and an I2C multiplexer
  • HX711 load cell for weight
  • ESP32 controller

    Sericulture words, explained

    Chandrika

    The mounting frame with small compartments where mature worms spin their cocoons.

    Instar

    A growth stage between moults. The silkworm passes through five instars as a larva.

    Deflossing

    Removing the loose outer fibre (floss) from a cocoon before reeling. It is part of our next concept.

    Stifling

    Using heat to kill the pupa so the moth cannot cut through the cocoon and break the filament.

    Reeling

    Unwinding cocoon filaments and combining them into raw silk thread.
